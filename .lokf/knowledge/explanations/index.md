# Explanations

* [Modelling choices](modelling-choices.md) - Why the schema has these shapes: records that refer by identifier, documented information under one document-control mixin, variants told apart by a designator, numbers for citing the standard, and scales offered for an organization that adopts them.
* [Mappings and the SSSOM sets](mappings.md) - Where the schema crosses to other vocabularies: close mappings to lmodel/iso27001's class of the same clause number, to OSCAL's role and SPDX's organization, and a related mapping to OSCAL's control, each tested. The seven SSSOM sets are gone.
* [Scope](scope.md) - What the model leaves out: the text of the standard, certification beyond the system's own record of an audit and its status, a fixed method of risk assessment, and the rules no validator checks.
